# Generation plan — lifestyle

Goal: a secondary image that places the product in a plausible home setting, without any
complex interaction, to support Nima's editorial/lifestyle visual language.

Composition rules:

- Product remains the clear focal point — do not shrink it into background clutter.
- Home context only (e.g. a floor, a windowsill, a countertop) that is consistent with the
  product's real scale (`scale_constraints`).
- A pet or person may be present only as ambient context (e.g. resting nearby, not gripping,
  operating, or wearing the product) — anything beyond ambient presence belongs in `in-use`,
  not here.
- Lighting: warm, natural, consistent with Nima's home-photography tone — no studio strobe look.

Mandatory rules:

- Same fidelity requirements as `refined` for shape/color/material/count/function — a lifestyle
  setting never licenses changing the product itself.
- If a pet is shown, its size must be plausible against `scale_constraints`; do not use a pet
  species/size that contradicts what the product is sized for.
- No invented accessories attached to the product.

Rejection criteria: product altered, product occluded beyond recognition, implausible scale,
or any interaction that goes beyond passive/ambient presence.
