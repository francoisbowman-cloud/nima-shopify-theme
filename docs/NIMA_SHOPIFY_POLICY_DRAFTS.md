# Nima — Shopify Policy Drafts

Operational launch drafts for merchant review before publishing in Shopify Admin. These are intentionally conservative and avoid promises that have not been verified in the live runtime. They are not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Shipping Policy — English

```html
<p>Nima currently ships to delivery addresses within the United States. Products in our active catalog are configured with free standard shipping at checkout.</p>
<p>Orders may be fulfilled by third-party fulfillment partners. Processing and delivery timing can vary by product, destination, carrier, and fulfillment partner. Any delivery estimate shown at checkout or in order communications is an estimate rather than a guaranteed arrival date.</p>
<p>When tracking information is available, it will be provided through Shopify order communications. Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address before placing an order.</p>
<p>If an order has a delivery problem, please contact us through the store contact form or at {{ email }} with your order number so we can review the shipment.</p>
```

## Refund Policy — English

```html
<p>If there is a problem with your order, please contact Nima promptly through the store contact form or at {{ email }} before sending any item back.</p>
<p>Return and refund eligibility depends on the item, its condition, the reason for the request, fulfillment requirements, and any rights that apply under applicable law. Items should not be returned without return instructions from Nima.</p>
<p>If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, contact us with the order number and supporting photos when possible so we can review the issue with the fulfillment partner.</p>
<p>When a refund is approved, it is issued to the original payment method through Shopify. Processing time after approval can vary by payment provider. Original shipping or return-shipping charges, when applicable, are handled according to the circumstances of the approved return and applicable law.</p>
<p>This policy does not limit rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.</p>
```

## Terms of Service — Launch baseline

Use Shopify's current Terms of Service template as the legal baseline in Admin and review it before publishing. At minimum, ensure it accurately identifies Nima as the merchant, the United States as the current shipping market, Shopify as the commerce platform, third-party fulfillment where applicable, payment at checkout, product/price availability, prohibited misuse, limitation language permitted by applicable law, governing-law/dispute language selected by the merchant, and contact details.

Do not add invented guarantees, fixed delivery windows, unconditional return windows, or unsupported warranty language.
